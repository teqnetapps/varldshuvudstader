Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 Sept 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Världens Huvudstäder handles information when you use the app.

The app is designed for children and educational purposes. We take children's privacy particularly seriously and have therefore designed the app so that it does not collect, store, or share personal information about its users.

1. No Collection of Personal Information

Världens Huvudstäder does not collect any personal information.

The app does not ask users to provide information such as:

name
email address
telephone number
home address
date of birth
photos or videos
location information
contact information
identifiers that can be used to identify an individual user

The app does not require users to create an account.

2. No Storage of User Data

The app does not store personal information on our servers or in any cloud service.

If applicable, certain settings or learning progress required for the app's functionality may be stored locally on the device on which the app is installed. This information is not transmitted to us.

If the app is uninstalled, locally stored information may be deleted, depending on the device and operating system.

3. No Tracking

Världens Huvudstäder does not use tracking technologies to monitor or follow users' activities.

The app does not use:

advertising IDs for profiling
behavioral tracking
third-party cookies
analytics services to identify individual users
social media tracking
user profiles

We do not use information about how an individual user interacts with the app for marketing or profiling purposes.

4. No Advertising

The app does not contain targeted advertising and does not use user data for advertising purposes.

[Adapt this section if the app contains advertising.]

5. Children's Privacy

The app is intended to be used by children for educational and learning purposes.

Because the app does not collect personal information, users do not need to provide any personal information in order to use the app's features.

We have designed the app with the principle that children should be able to use it without having to disclose personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

The operating system and app store may nevertheless process technical information in connection with the installation and use of the app in accordance with their own privacy policies. Such processing is carried out by the respective providers and is outside our control.

7. Data Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information to us, we do not hold personal information that can be sold, shared, used for profiling, or disclosed to third parties.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

Since Världens Huvudstäder does not collect or store personal information about its users, there is generally no personal information held by us that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality or the way information is handled changes.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will always be available at:

https://teqnetapps.github.io/varldshuvudstader/privacy.md

The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this policy.
